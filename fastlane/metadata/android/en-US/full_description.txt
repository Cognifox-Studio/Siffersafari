Siffersafari is an offline-first math adventure for children in grades 1–9 (about ages 6–12).

Children can:
- play short quizzes in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division
- train by grade level with adaptive difficulty
- earn points, badges, and camp souvenirs while following a jungle story map

Adults can:
- create multiple child profiles on the same device
- unlock parent mode with a PIN
- review local progress and control content without interrupting play

Why Siffersafari:
- no account and no internet required for the core loop
- progress stays on the device
- no ads
- no tracking
- built for a simple, COPPA-minded child flow

Daily math practice that feels playful, clear, and easy to start.
