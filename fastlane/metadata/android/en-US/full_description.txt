Siffersafari is an offline-first math game for children ages 6-12.

Children can:
- play short quizzes in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division
- choose operations and difficulty based on age and progress
- collect points, rewards, and follow their story through the app

Adults can:
- create multiple child profiles on the same device
- unlock parent mode with a PIN
- review progress and control content without disturbing the child flow

Why Siffersafari:
- works without an account and without requiring internet access
- stores progress locally on the device
- no ads
- no tracking
- built for a simple and child-friendly experience

The app is designed for daily math practice that feels playful, clear, and easy to start.
